Untitled canvas

Tool buttons are 2.25rem square at 0.5rem radius: transparent at rest, --accent on hover, --primary when active. Floating chips and panels sit on --card with a 1px --border and --shadow-panel. Panel radius is 0.75rem.

Surface note

The drawing host itself is pure white (#fff), not paper. The warm palette is the chrome around the canvas; the canvas is a white sheet. Anything drawn as an overlay on the artwork — selection included — is judged against white, not against --paper.

Off-palette today

The selection outline in Renderer.js is hard-coded to #4f46e5, a cold indigo with no relationship to any token above. It predates this palette. Every option in the Selection group uses --primary (terracotta) or --ink instead, which is a visible change from what currently ships.
