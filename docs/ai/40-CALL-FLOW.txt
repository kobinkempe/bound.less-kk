ERASING   [HOT at gesture time, deferred for the geometry]

  pointerdown with tool "erasePartial"
    _pointerDown -> a stroke with erase:true, colour #ffffff
                    (the gesture costs nothing; z-order makes it correct)
  ...later, from _bakeTickInner - one cut or one descent in flight at a time
  (DESIGN.md section 7, 2026-09-08):
    _eraseFramesPass(Erec)                a frame the mark covers whole (cell +
                                          reach inside its ink, nothing drawn
                                          above the mark) goes as containers,
                                          one bake step, no cut
    _nextEraseTarget(Erec)                from _candidatesUnder: the spatial index
                                          of every reachable level, z descending
    _bakeOne(Erec, target)
      if the target is a stroke      -> _forceBake it first
      if the target is a legacy fill -> doc.fillToShapeById
      homed shallower, on an ancestor chain:                     CEDE
        _bakeRehome -> _bakeRehomeInner: the target, the eraser and lm.serialize()
          runDescentAsync (the erase worker) or descentJob inline:
            on a VirtualDoc, one crossing at a time: squareInkOf (the level's
            square ink as the tile store derives it, F42), cedeVirtual (the
            parent cut in its own coordinates)
          -> _onCutResult -> _applyDescent: lm.merge(the frames the job minted,
             F67), the family minted with real ids, the steps on the op,
             _resplitFamily
      else:                                                      CUT IN PLACE
        _eraserLoopsInto(loops, HE, o, HO)  the eraser in the target's frame,
                                            a moved object's table inverted
        runCutAsync (the erase worker) or cutJob inline: the boolean centred on
          the SUBJECT, the removal measured locally and the graze rule,
          shapeComponents, dropDust (a quarter pixel at the mark's zoom)
        -> _onCutResult -> _applyCut: doc.eraseReplaceById, _resplitFamily if
           it split; a result whose target changed (_ver) is dropped
      _lowerMark(Erec, target)            the mark's z steps below what it handled
      (the cut and the step run in _neutral: their events reach _drainPlates tagged
       `neutral` and stale no plate - the mark already paints what they change, F90)
      (a step past a target left whole over ink is not neutral; a graze or an inkless
       descent refusal leaves nothing whole, F109)
    a mark with nothing left under it: _noteSpent, consumed (its removal stales its plates once)
  the barrier: _flushErasesFor(id) / _settleSelectionErases() run the same jobs
    inline before a select or a drag; _flushErasesFor steps the mark (_lowerMark)
    when what it bakes is the mark's next target; _settleLassoErases(poly) bakes
    out every mark the loop meets before a lasso is judged (flushErases(ids));
    a mark whose cut is in the worker is never spent by a barrier: flushErases,
    and _flushErasesFor before it bakes another object of that mark, run that
    cut inline first (_settleInflight); the drag's barrier skips such a mark
  only the pen's holder bakes: a read-only engine's tick polls (500 ms) until it
    has the pen; a destroyed engine drops a late worker answer (OPEN-FLAGS F96)

  SEVERANCE
    _resplitFamily(op, key)
      _familyComponents(key)
        _familyMembers(key)                 every native sharing the editId
        for each member with an attachRect:
          contactArcs(_inkOutline(kid), R, tol)
          for each member one level coarser, touching:
            lm.mapRectF(R, kid.level, up.level)
            arcsTouch(kidArcs, upArcs, tol)  -> Groups.union
      re-label each component with a fresh editId; no geometry is touched

THE SCALE BAR - a pure function of zoom, computed in React

  ScaleHud render                           engine.onZoom(setZoom): once a frame, a
                                            zoom re-renders this leaf, not the editor
    computeScale(effectiveZoom, scaleDef, session)
      mppFromDef(scaleDef, zoom)            metres per pixel, on the log spine
      clearUserBandIfExited(session, mpp)
      resolveReading(mpp, session)
        ladder(id).candidatesAt(mpp)        every grammar-legal stop that fits
        collapse to ONE representative per unit
        rank: user range, then preference band, then LOWEST IN-RANGE NUMBER,
              then bar-target closeness
      withReading(session, reading)
    a unit pick instead goes through applyUnitPick, which either switches ladder
    or installs a user range whose edges are found by SCANNING the resolver
