WHERE THE BUILD IS

    The arc pipeline (F21/F22) and the frame lattice are both IN and are the
    engine. A stroke is drawn as a chain of circular arcs and resolves at pen-up
    into a perimeter of arcs, which becomes the object; the centerline is then
    deleted. Erasing is one exact boolean on two arc perimeters, with no polygon
    library anywhere on that path. A frame is a cell of a fixed lattice, so a
    move is arithmetic on an address rather than a translation of geometry, and
    the magnify chain carries exact arcs down instead of a polygon.

    That closed F25, F28, F-A and F-B. It did not close the eraser.

    Cloud sync is live: Google auth, Firestore, chunked lz1 payloads, thumbnails
    on the parent doc, a soft-delete recycle bin, and a presence heartbeat that
    warns when the same canvas is open on another device.

OPEN, IN THE ORDER THEY MATTER

    F33  autosave dies silently when localStorage fills          HIGH, MITIGATED
         LOCAL AUTOSAVE IS OFF. LOCAL_AUTOSAVE = false in hooks/useKobinEngine.js;
         flip it to true to restore and nothing else has to change. The Save
         button and cloud sync still work, and a standing dismissible banner says
         autosave is off.
         Two things were fixed on the way and are worth knowing, because they
         mean a signed-in user with full storage previously had NO working save
         path at all: a failed local write used to return before reaching
         cloudSaveCanvas, and cloudDirtyRef was only ever set by a SUCCESSFUL
         local autosave.
         WHAT IS ACTUALLY LEFT IS NOT THE QUOTA. Measured on a phone: five
         autosaves that all SUCCEEDED cost 29.6 s of a 48.6 s window, 97% of it
         inside LZString.compressToUTF16 on a 16-million-character document.
         Compressing that on the main thread is unaffordable at any quota. The
         fix has to be a worker, or IndexedDB (no 5 MB cap, takes structured
         data), or incremental saves. Unbounded thumbnails (1.54 MB across 251
         keys on his machine) buy headroom but do not make it fast.

    F34  an erase seals chords across the shape                  FIXED, UNVERIFIED
         Root cause found and fixed: circleCircle computed h2 = r1^2 - a^2, which
         is pure noise once r1 is ~1e10 and r2 is ~21, so crossings were lost or
         halved and the fragments could not pair. Kobin's own reproduction goes
         from 6 failures in 21 gestures to 0 of 21. HE HAS NOT SEEN IT IN A
         BROWSER YET. Do not close it on the arithmetic fix alone. Two things
         remain:
           - a synthetic straddling sweep at r >= 1e10 now finds all four
             crossings and STILL fails to assemble (146 open chains). Different
             fault, same regime.
           - the DESIGN-CORRECT repair, which Kobin is right about: the freeze is
             wired into the render chain only, so the erase path consults a curve
             the design says no longer exists. Either freeze on the cede path, or
             intersect in bulge form and never materialise the centre.

    F35  a group move displaces one member                       HIGH, CAPTURED
         Six reports captured across an undo/redo of the move; snapshots in
         .kobin-reports/f35/, renders in public/__f35/ (gitignored). Every report
         is a loadable drawing - src/engine/f35.render.test.js is the reader, and
         that applies to EVERY report ever captured, not just these.
         Not diagnosed. The cheapest thing to rule out first is not arithmetic:
         three silent `continue`s in _dragSelection each drop ONE member of a
         drag while every other member moves on, and nothing logs any of them.
         Two unexplained facts from the numbers: the relative offset between
         members CHANGES across an undo/redo with no gesture in between, and the
         frame chain is rewritten between two of the reports.

    F29  a piece fades out of existence while zooming out        HIGH
         Caught mid-fade, after being cut off the main piece. Not reproduced.
         Two candidates, both cheap to check: fadeTag is projectedSizePx at the
         SOURCE frame's deepest zoom, so a freshly ceded piece reads much smaller
         than it looks; and a piece that is actually fading is put in its own
         render group rather than the family's, which makes the fade visible
         instead of masked. Harness: report 07-22-12 plus a zoom-out sweep.

    F30  an eraser is consumed without cutting anything          HIGH
         The first cede at a new level did nothing. Does NOT reproduce
         synchronously - the replay path runs flushErases() and the app runs
         _bakeTick in 8 ms slices. The suspect is the DONE SET: _bakeOne marks a
         target done BEFORE it can refuse, so a refusal is permanent for that
         gesture. Instrumented: _rehomeBail records every refusal with its
         reason, _noteSpent records the whole done set of a mark consumed
         without cutting. A repeat now arrives with its own explanation attached.

    F32  the lasso sometimes misses objects                      OPEN
         Reported, never reproduced, no capture. Two things to rule out first,
         both read off the code rather than observed: selection tests an object's
         AXIS-ALIGNED RECT, not its ink, so a diagonal or curved stroke lying
         comfortably inside the loop is skipped whenever its bbox corners poke
         out - correct by the stated rule, a miss to the person drawing the loop;
         and _rectInActive returning null drops an object silently. Ask for a
         report while it is on screen.

WHAT IS DELIBERATELY NOT BUILT
    - D1 from the frame-lattice bible: a piece is still {C, r, a0, sweep, A, B}
      rather than {A, B, bulge}. The freeze TEST never touches the centre, and a
      line is never chopped, so an arc is only ever chopped at shallow depths -
      but that is a structural argument standing in for a representation change.
      It is the last decision from section 0 outstanding, and F34's root cause is
      exactly the class of failure it would have prevented.
    - The touching-arcs severance case (frame-lattice bible §7.4). Scoped,
      understood, agreed not to build.
    - The seam antialiasing rule (§7.5). A look-at-it question, nothing measured.
    - Frame garbage collection. Abandoned cells are re-findable now, so the leak
      is harmless, but nothing collects them.
    - Threading. The bake is small enough that a worker buys little; the real
      cost is asynchrony against a mutable document.
