STORAGE AND CLOUD

src/storage/db.js              IndexedDB, and why (F33, in the header comment).
                               Version 2 (2026-09-08, the kobin-2 store): a
                               header record per canvas, a frames2 record per
                               frame (object headers + the raw Float64Array
                               buffer), a log record per op-log entry; putCanvas2
                               writes the snapshots it is given, appends entries,
                               drops entries under the compaction floor, and on a
                               full write deletes the canvas's v1 frames; getCanvas2
                               returns the store or `{legacy}` for a v1 canvas.
                               Thumbnails (JPEG bytes, LRU budget), the recycle-bin
                               payloads and the pre-pull backups keep their stores.
src/storage/localCanvases.js   The store's public face. The gallery index and
                               the recycle-bin index stay in localStorage
                               (synchronous, a few KB); everything that touches
                               a document is async. sweepStorage is the list of
                               what used to leak: orphan documents (adopted as
                               drafts if drawn on, deleted if empty), orphan
                               frames, backups older than a week, thumbnails for
                               canvases that exist nowhere. stashOverwrittenVersion
                               files the losing side of a cross-device overwrite
                               as its own restorable canvas. A kobin-2 canvas
                               goes to the bin in place (`store: true`), and is
                               duplicated and backed up as a whole-store copy
                               (F86); loadCanvasDoc is the whole drawing.
src/storage/foldStore.js       A stored kobin-2 canvas as ONE kobin-1 document
                               (F86): the frame snapshots, plus the log tail
                               replayed through a detached engine as the editor
                               loads it; no engine when there is no tail, the
                               snapshots alone when the log will not replay. Used
                               by loadCanvasDoc and cloudLoadCanvas.
src/storage/thumbnails.js      Scene thumbnails. renderThumbsLive (the raster
                               path, 2026-09-22/23): sceneOps.snapshotView per
                               scene, at most four in flight, idle-spaced, each
                               landing through onThumb. withSvgEngine: a one-off
                               SVG KobinEngine in a hidden host over a serialised
                               copy - what renderThumbs (?svg only) and the SVG
                               export use. Browser-only.
src/cloud/firebaseApp.js       Lazy, memoized Firebase init. Importing it starts
                               nothing.
src/cloud/useUser.js           Live auth state; Google popup with a redirect
                               fallback for phones that block popups.
src/cloud/lzWorker.js          lz-string in a Web Worker (public/lz-worker.js,
                               with lz-string 1.5.0 vendored beside it), falling
                               back to the main thread where there is no worker.
                               The cloud payload's compression was 97% of the
                               F33 freeze and the 30 s sync ran it every time.
src/cloud/gzip.js              CompressionStream("gzip") for the cloud chunks,
                               raw where it is missing, injectable for tests
                               (Node's zlib). Never lz-string (F64).
src/cloud/store2.js            The kobin-2 store as chunks: a frame snapshot or a
                               run of log entries is one format2 chunk, split into
                               700 KiB parts. Pure; store2.test.js.
src/cloud/canvasSync.js        Firestore, under users/{uid}/<col>: `canvases` only
                               in a production build on the deployed app's hosts
                               (REAL_HOSTS), `dev-canvases` everywhere else
                               (isDevBuild; SYNC-PLAN R7).
                               kobin-2 (2026-09-08): the parent doc is the
                               MANIFEST (frames {fid: chunk, seq}, log chunks,
                               head {epoch, seq}, lease, listing fields) and
                               parts/{chunk~i} hold the gzipped chunks.
                               cloudPushCanvas2 (2026-09-22) sends what the store's
                               one-transaction read (readPushState) says the cloud
                               lacks, one part a commit, in the page's one push
                               lane (inLane, with cloudSaveCanvas: the write
                               stream refuses overlapping uploads; a push with
                               no progress for its deadline lets the lane go and
                               is marked dead), stopping at the next part once
                               another tab claims the store,
                               parts named by epoch and a per-push nonce,
                               the manifest in a transaction that refuses unless
                               the head is the local base and the lease is ours
                               (SyncConflict / LeaseLost); cloudPullCanvas2 pulls
                               whole or fast-forward. Lease: cloudTakeLease,
                               cloudRenewLease, cloudReleaseLease (`to` the asker),
                               cloudAskLease; cloudWatchManifest (onSnapshot).
                               Legacy lz1: parent doc carries metadata and
                               thumbnails; the drawing is lz1-compressed and
                               chunked into 700 KiB binary parts underneath, six
                               parts per commit. THE PARENT IS WRITTEN LAST,
                               because readers key off parent.parts/codec, so a
                               torn save never looks complete. Soft delete is a
                               deletedAt on the parent; a later full save clears
                               it, so editing anywhere resurrects a canvas.
                               (The presence heartbeat went 2026-09-22.)
src/cloud/syncCore.js          Pure rules of SYNC-PLAN: headOf, sameHead,
                               leaseState, decideOpen (sync / push / pull /
                               conflict / stash / legacy), conflictName,
                               defaultDeviceName. syncCore.test.js.
src/cloud/canvasSession.js     One open editor's part in one-editor-at-a-time:

src/cloud/storageLocks.js      navigator.locks over localStorage, where there are

THE ENGINE - src/engine/

eraseRule.js        THE ERASE RULE (A2 §4.1, 2026-09-28): underMask(o, M) — o is
                    under mark M's mask iff M landed (targets), M in o.targetOf,
                    M not in o.handledBy, z(o) < z(M); read by the pack, the
                    bake, the barriers, the pick and the Stage's step class
                    (inLineage). inheritErase: a piece's lineage at the three
                    mint sites. Sorted id arrays (has, withId, withoutId). Pure.
cede.js             CEDE A TILE: the one body of Document.cedeTileById and the
                    descent's virtual cede (R25 D4): the parent's hole cut
                    exactly, remnants and kids in one family with the lineage
                    inherited, the remnant's dust boxes on its removal.
eraseJob.js         ONE CUT AS A JOB (2026-09-08): cutJob({subject, clip, w,
                    freezeR, graze}) — the boolean, the removal measured locally
                    (F46), the graze rule ("cut" in place / "cede" the rehome's),
                    shapeComponents and dropDust; result {kind: grazed|removed|
                    cut, regions as Loops, stats, dust, dustBoxes}. wireJob/unwireJob
                    and wireResult/unwireResult carry it between threads as
                    transferable buffers, bit for bit. Geometry imports only.
eraseWorkerClient.js THE CUTS IN THE SHARED POOL (B-PYRAMID-2b, 2026-09-29; PLAN
                    §1.6): submit(queue, {kind, barrier, job}) -> a promise of the
                    cut's or the descent's result, one job of kind "cut" at
                    PRIO.cut through a JobQueue (the renderer's, or cutQueue() on
                    the SVG path) posted to a raster worker (runCut); null when the
                    pool is broken or there is no worker (the pipeline runs the job
                    inline). While any cut is pending the queue keeps one worker
                    for cuts. A job a worker error lost is requeued once; the one
                    the watchdog took, or a second loss, rejects with err.why and
                    the pipeline refuses the pair over ink. cutSlots(queue) bounds
                    the lookahead; eraseWorkerStats() counts.
eraseDescent.js     THE DESCENT AS ONE JOB (2026-09-08, Kobin's design):
                    descentJob(world, input) runs the rehome descent on a
                    VirtualDoc (the target read, every cede virtual, negative
                    ids), squareInkOf deriving each level's square ink as
                    TileStore._bakeUp does for that one object, cede.js the
                    parent cut; returns steps with their dust boxes (plus `minted`, the frames
                    the job's lattice copy gained, F67) or a refusal. wireDescent /
                    unwireDescent carry the target, the eraser and the lattice
                    (LevelMap.serialize) to the worker; _applyDescent in
                    erasePipeline swaps the family in with real ids. A test
                    puts `squareInk` on the world to watch or fault it.
worker/rasterWorker.js  the raster worker's entry, bundled by `npm run build:worker`
                    (esbuild, iife, es2019) into public/raster-worker.js, run
                    before start and build: one strip of plates a message
                    (rasterCore.renderPlate per plate; each plate's pixels, a
                    bitmap or a pixel block, timings, counts and the job's guard
                    words go back), or one cut or descent ("cut-job": runCut).
                    Knows no document, camera or canvas.

LevelMap.js         THE FRAME LATTICE. Frames, their cells and edges, and the
                    pure transforms between them. tileRect/tileRange are the
                    cache square, TileGrid.CACHE's rect and `touching` (the
                    closed read, frameLattice.js) since 2026-09-07.
                    cellChild/neighbour do the
                    carry arithmetic; merge adopts frames another copy minted
                    and ensureId mints the frame an id names (F67, 2026-09-08);
                    displaceFrame is the whole of a deep move;
                    framePath/frameFactor/mapRectF/projectF are the transforms,
                    and they go ONE EDGE AT A TIME because a composed long jump
                    cancels catastrophically. _paths caches the tree walk, which
                    dominated a pan on a seventeen-level document. projectF is
                    a second copy of the object-to-level projection (derive.js
                    deriveStep is the one the screen shows) and past five
                    crossings their last bits part by 4096x per crossing; since
                    F42 the erase no longer uses it for the target's ink (it
                    reads the tile store), and it serves the down path, the
                    ring natives and the eraser. objShift (the displacement
                    table read at a frame: the frame the picture is fetched
                    from plus the sub-frame remainder paint applies) and
                    mapPointObj / mapRectObj / projectLoopsObj, the transforms
                    that add that remainder on the way out of an object's
                    frame and subtract it on the way in (F41, F55). Since
                    2026-09-15 a frame's id is fixed and its address mutable
